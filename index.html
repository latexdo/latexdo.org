Questions, answered.

Is LatexDo free?

Yes. LatexDo is free to download and use, and its source code is open under the MIT license. It is built by a French nonprofit association and funded by donations.

Does LatexDo work offline?

Yes. Your projects are ordinary folders on your disk and are compiled on your own machine. A local AI model also works with no internet once it has been downloaded. Cloud AI providers are optional.

Which operating systems are supported?

LatexDo runs on macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows (64-bit) and Linux (64-bit AppImage).

Do I need LaTeX installed?

LatexDo compiles with the TeX distribution on your computer: MacTeX on macOS, MiKTeX or TeX Live on Windows, and TeX Live on Linux.

Can I move my paper from Overleaf or Word?

Yes. You can import an Overleaf project, a Word document, a Markdown file or a searchable PDF and keep working in LaTeX.

Is my paper uploaded anywhere?

No. LatexDo needs no account and your files stay on your computer. If you choose to connect a cloud AI provider, only the text you send to the assistant goes to that provider.
