Mission

Help more people produce high-quality technical documents.

LaTeX is central to science, engineering, mathematics, and technical publishing, but the tooling can be difficult for new users and costly for teams that need reliable workflows. LatexDo exists to keep a modern, open, inspectable option available to the public.

Students

Lower the learning curve for reports, theses, lab notes, and early research papers.

Researchers

Improve draft, review, rebuttal, citation, and submission workflows for academic work.

Educators

Provide free material that can be used in classes, workshops, and writing support.

Maintainers

Keep source code, roadmap decisions, and infrastructure costs visible to contributors.
