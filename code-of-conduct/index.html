Code of Conduct

Work with respect and clarity.

LatexDo contributors, team members, and community participants are expected to keep collaboration respectful, focused, and useful.

Respectful collaboration

Treat other people with respect. Do not harass, insult, threaten, or exclude people based on identity, background, level of experience, or technical disagreement.

Practical technical work

Discuss ideas directly and constructively. Keep decisions grounded in evidence, project goals, user needs, maintainability, and the quality of the LatexDo experience.

Reporting concerns

Concerns about conduct can be sent to contact@latexdo.org. The LatexDo team may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
