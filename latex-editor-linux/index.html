LatexDo for Linux

The LaTeX editor for Linux.

Free and open source. One AppImage for 64-bit Linux. Write, compile, cite and review in one window.

Download for Linux
Install

Make it executable and run.

Download the AppImage, then in a terminal:

chmod +x LatexDo-linux-x64.AppImage
./LatexDo-linux-x64.AppImage
LaTeX

Uses TeX Live.

LatexDo compiles with TeX Live and latexmk from your distribution. Install them once and every project builds locally.

Systems

64-bit, recent distributions.

Built for x86-64 systems with glibc 2.34 or newer, such as Ubuntu 22.04 and later.

What you get

Everything your paper needs.

Source and PDF side by side

Click a sentence in the PDF to land on its source line, or go the other way.

Errors in plain language

A failed build tells you what went wrong, where, and what to do.

Citations and a knowledge graph

Search Crossref and OpenAlex and see how your references connect.

AI on your terms

Run a model locally, connect Ollama, or bring your own provider. Edits wait for your approval.

Review and rebuttal

Annotate a paper as a reviewer, or answer each comment next to the manuscript.
