Contact

Reach the LatexDo association.

Use the official addresses below for nonprofit, governance, donation, privacy, security, accessibility, and contribution questions. Please do not send payment card details or private documents by email.

General contact

For general questions, press requests, educational use, nonprofit eligibility questions, and help finding the right contact, email contact@latexdo.org.

Board and governance

Questions about association governance, public records, donations, expenses, and roadmap decisions can be sent to board@latexdo.org.

Open-source participation

Bugs, documentation improvements, and code contributions are tracked through the public source repository at github.com/latexdo/latexdo. Contributors who prefer email can send a short description to contact@latexdo.org.

Privacy

Privacy questions can be sent by filling out this form.

Security

Security reports should include the affected URL, version, or repository path and enough detail for the maintainers to reproduce the issue. Send the report by filling out this form.
