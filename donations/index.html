Questions

Frequently asked.

Is LatexDo really free?

Yes. The desktop app has no paid tier. LatexDo is and will remain free. Donations support development, but there is no paywall.

What makes LatexDo different from Overleaf?

LatexDo is open source from day one. The desktop app compiles using your local LaTeX installation, keeps projects on your machine, and avoids a subscription workflow. The nonprofit mission is to keep practical LaTeX tooling available for the public.
