Questions

Frequently asked.

Is LatexDo really free?

Yes. The web editor requires no account, and the desktop app has no paid tier. LatexDo is and will remain free. Donations support development, but there is no paywall.

What makes LatexDo different from Overleaf?

LatexDo is open source from day one — no enterprise upsell, no hidden features behind a subscription. The desktop app compiles using your local LaTeX installation, and the web editor needs no account. There is no VC-backed growth play here; it is just a good LaTeX editor.

Do you accept one-time donations?

Yes. Contact contact@latexdo.org and we will send the available donation options. Every amount helps, whether a single gift or ongoing support.

LatexDo vs LatexDo Pro

LatexDo is the free, open-source LaTeX editor. LatexDo Pro is the Enterprise version with optimized for Enterprise users. Contact board@latexdo.org for more information.
