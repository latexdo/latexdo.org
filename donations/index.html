Questions

Frequently asked.

Is LatexDo really free?

Yes. The desktop app has no paid tier. LatexDo is and will remain free. Donations support development, but there is no paywall.

What makes LatexDo different from Overleaf?

LatexDo is open source from day one. The desktop app compiles using your local LaTeX installation, keeps projects on your machine, and avoids a subscription workflow. The nonprofit mission is to keep practical LaTeX tooling available for the public.

Do you accept one-time donations?

Yes. Contact contact@latexdo.org and the association will send the currently available secure donation or bank transfer options. Every amount helps, whether a single gift or ongoing support.

Is there a paid version?

The public LatexDo desktop app and source code are free and open source. The nonprofit website focuses on the public mission, documentation, research workflows, and community support.

Can I support LatexDo without donating?

Yes. You can report bugs, improve documentation, test releases, share LatexDo with students or researchers, or contact the association about educational use.
