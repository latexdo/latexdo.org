Why donate to LatexDo?

It is completely open source

Every line of LatexDo's source code is public on GitHub. No paywalls, no proprietary licenses, no hidden features. Your donations ensure this stays true.

Built for the academic community

LatexDo serves researchers, students, reviewers, and technical writers who need a reliable LaTeX workflow. Donations help us focus on the tools that matter for serious writing.

Infrastructure and hosting costs

The web editor, download servers, and update infrastructure all have real operating costs. Your support keeps the service running smoothly for everyone.

Sustained development

New features, bug fixes, and community support take time and attention. Donations allow us to dedicate more effort to making LatexDo better for every user.

LatexDo

Modern LaTeX editing, open for everyone.

LatexDo is a focused LaTeX editor for writing papers, compiling PDFs, managing citations, reviewing comments, preparing rebuttals, and working across web and desktop. It is free, open source, and built for researchers, students, and technical writers who need a reliable workflow from draft to submission.
