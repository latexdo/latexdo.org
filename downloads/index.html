Free nonprofit software

Download LatexDo desktop app.

LatexDo is a free, open-source desktop app for local-first LaTeX writing. It is maintained by the LatexDo nonprofit association for students, researchers, educators, reviewers, and technical writers.

What you need

LatexDo compiles with your own TeX toolchain. Install a TeX distribution such as TeX Live, MacTeX, or MiKTeX if your computer does not already have LaTeX available.
