About the association

A nonprofit building free LaTeX tools.

LATEXDO is a declared French nonprofit association governed by the law of July 1, 1901. It builds and maintains free, open-source LaTeX tools, documentation, and research workflows for students, researchers, educators, reviewers, and technical writers.

Mission

The association exists to make scientific and technical writing with LaTeX more accessible, reliable, and transparent. LaTeX is essential in academic and technical publishing, but installation, compilation, bibliography management, reviewer responses, and submission checks remain difficult for many users.

LATEXDO addresses these needs through public software, open-source code, and practical resources that anyone can inspect, use, and improve.

Finances and transparency

LATEXDO is funded by donations and volunteer contributions, and the application stays free. You can see all expenses on the expenses page.

Legal framework and governance

Governance questions, association records, donations, and roadmap decisions are handled by the LATEXDO board. Requests can be sent through the contact page.

Reference texts

The main applicable text is the French Law of July 1, 1901 on associations. It defines the framework for nonprofit associations in France.

The main implementing text is the French Decree of August 16, 1901 implementing the law of July 1, 1901. It sets out the practical rules for declaration, publication, and administrative formalities.
