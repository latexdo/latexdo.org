Mission

The association exists to make scientific and technical writing with LaTeX more accessible, reliable, and transparent. LaTeX is essential in academic and technical publishing, but installation, compilation, bibliography management, reviewer responses, and submission checks remain difficult for many users.

LATEXDO addresses these needs through public software, open-source code, and practical resources that anyone can inspect, use, and improve.

Activities / Achievements

LATEXDO has developed and published a free desktop application for working on LaTeX projects locally, with source editing, PDF preview, local compilation, and technical document workflows.

The association also publishes latexdo.org, documentation, research pages about the compiler, knowledge graphs, and AI workflows, plus operating expense information for donors and contributors.

Current Projects

LATEXDO is currently working to stabilize desktop releases, downloads, and release verification, improve public documentation, and strengthen research workflows around LaTeX compilation, AI, and knowledge graphs.

The current work remains aligned with the mission: provide open, free, and inspectable tools for producing reliable scientific, educational, and technical documents.

Reference Texts

The main applicable text is the French Law of July 1, 1901 on associations. It defines the framework for nonprofit associations in France.

The main implementing text is the French Decree of August 16, 1901 implementing the law of July 1, 1901. It sets out the practical rules for declaration, publication, and administrative formalities.
