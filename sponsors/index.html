Sponsors

Sponsor free LaTeX tools.

LatexDo is a free, open-source LaTeX editor run by a French nonprofit association. Sponsors keep it free for students, researchers, and teachers everywhere.

What your sponsorship funds

  • Development and maintenance of the desktop editor on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
  • Infrastructure: the website, downloads, update feeds, and the extension store.
  • Code signing, security reviews, and testing.
  • Documentation, translations, and community support.

The association publishes its spending on the expenses page.

What sponsors receive

  • Your organization's name and logo on this page, linked to your website.
  • A public thank-you in the release notes and on the LatexDo blog.
  • A mention in the association's annual activity report.
  • A direct contact with the maintainers for feedback on how your team uses LatexDo.

We agree on the amount, duration, and recognition with each sponsor. Write to us and we will send you the details.

What sponsorship does not buy

LatexDo stays independent. Sponsors do not control the roadmap, the code, or the association's decisions, and LatexDo will never show ads or sell user data. The editor stays free and open source under the MIT License.
