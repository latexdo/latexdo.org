Questions

Frequently asked.

Can I sponsor LatexDo as an individual?

Yes. Individuals can support LatexDo with a one-time or monthly donation of any amount.

Can we choose an amount that is not listed?

Yes. The tiers are a starting point. Tell us your budget and we will build a sponsorship around it.

Do sponsors get access to a special version of LatexDo?

No. Everyone gets the same free app. Sponsors get recognition, contact with the team, and the services listed above.

Is our sponsorship tax-deductible?

It depends on your country and on how the sponsorship is structured. Write to us and we will explain the options before you commit.

Can a university or a research lab sponsor LatexDo?

Yes, and the official thesis template and the student workshop are made for you. Ask us for an education package.
