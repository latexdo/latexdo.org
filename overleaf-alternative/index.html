Your paper, on your own computer.

Choose LatexDo

When the paper should stay with you.

You work on trains, planes or behind a firewall. Your project is confidential or under embargo. Your document is large and hits compile limits. You want an assistant that runs on your own machine.

Choose Overleaf

When everyone types at once.

Overleaf is built for several authors editing the same document live in a browser, with nothing to install. If that is how your group writes, it is the right tool.

Use both

Bring your Overleaf project with you.

LatexDo imports an Overleaf project, so you can keep writing on your own computer. Because your project is a normal folder, it also works with Git.
